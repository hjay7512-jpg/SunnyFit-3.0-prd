08 / Methodology

Calculation notes and limitations

Segment denominator

Chat users/day = DAU × Chat adoption rate. Heavy, Moderate, and Light shares divide this group. No Chat is the remaining DAU; these users may still incur Home and Workout Result costs. Shares are normalized if they do not add to 100%.

Cost formula

Daily calls by feature = Σ(segment users × feature calls per user-day). Monthly model expense = Σ(daily calls × per-call rate) × days × (1 + budget contingency). This uses average daily patterns, not monthly unique accounts.

Controlled cache benchmark, Nov 11

Gemini: planning $0.011338, short Chat $0.011154, Home $0.002691. Luna: planning $0.005347, short Chat $0.005995, Home $0.000800. Home had 0% cache hits in both test runs.

Cost caveats

Workout Result proxies: Gemini $0.002700, Luna $0.001100 (not separately verified with real cache hits). Luna was not validated as a production-wide migration; latency and fallbacks require further evaluation. Confirm vendor prices and real invoiced costs before budget approval.
